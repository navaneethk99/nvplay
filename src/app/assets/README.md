`tmdb.png` is an unmodified rasterization of TMDB's approved primary logo,
https://www.themoviedb.org/assets/v4/logos/v2/blue_short-8e7b30f73a4020692ccca9c88bafe5dcb6f8a62a4c6bc55cd9ba82bb2cd95f6c.svg

Brand attribution: https://www.themoviedb.org/about/logos-attribution

`nvplay.svg` is the app icon supplied by the project owner. `nvplay.png` (256px)
and `nvplay.icns` are generated from it with `sh scripts/generate-icons-macos.sh`.
The PNG is embedded for window icons; the ICNS is copied into every macOS app
bundle and declared in Info.plist, so builds and updates retain the icon.

`nvplay-mark.svg` is the owner's transparent logo for the empty/stopped video
surface. `nvplay-mark.png` is its native-size rasterization, regenerated by
`scripts/generate-icons-macos.sh`. It replaces the previous traffic-cone drawing.
